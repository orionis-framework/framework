View In Hierarchy

Keep deferred effects and their completion inside an operation ledger.

Method __init__ Initialize an incident with its original exception and delivery status.
Method abandon Mark unstarted delivery as abandoned and notify waiting owners.
Method finish Store the selected handling plan and notify incident waiters.
Method reclaim Reset an abandoned incident for another delivery attempt.
Async Method wait Await incident completion without cancelling its delivery.
Class Variable __slots__ Incident fields retained only for the owning operation's lifetime.
