class AuthorizationSnapshot(IAuthorizationSnapshot): (source)

Constructor: AuthorizationSnapshot(permissions, roles, abilities)

View In Hierarchy

Hold the immutable authorization picture of a single request.

Effective authorization is the intersection of what the identity owns and what the presented credential is allowed to use:

effective = identity permissions ∩ credential abilities

A credential without abilities (None) never narrows the identity, and abilities alone can never grant a permission the identity lacks.

Concurrency

Instances are fully immutable once built, so they can be shared by every coroutine handling the same request without synchronisation.
