class BoundedQueueHandler(logging.Handler): (source)

Constructor: BoundedQueueHandler(target, capacity, app)

View In Hierarchy

Deliver records to an owned sink through a bounded background queue.

Notes

Reject full queues with queue.Full at every severity. Drain accepted records on close and surface background delivery failures to the caller.
